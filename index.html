About Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. The story follows humanity's struggle for survival against giant humanoid creatures known as Titans.

The Story

Set in a world where humanity is forced to live behind enormous walls to protect themselves from Titans, the series follows Eren Yeager and his friends as they join the military to fight against these mysterious creatures.

The World

The story takes place in a medieval-like world where humanity has built three concentric walls: Wall Maria, Wall Rose, and Wall Sheena, to protect themselves from the Titans that roam outside.

The Characters

Follow the journey of Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman, and Armin Arlert as they uncover the truth about the Titans and fight for humanity's freedom.
